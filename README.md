# Footwear Retail Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to practice end-to-end data analysis — from data modeling to visualization — using a footwear retail sales dataset.

---

## About This Project

This is a personal project I built to apply my Power BI and data analysis skills on a real-world style dataset. The dataset covers **4 years (2017–2020)** of footwear sales — **8,044 transactions** across **20 products, 4 brands, and 3 sales channels (Store, Online, Referral Program)** in multiple countries.

I wanted to go beyond just making charts — the goal was to understand the business, ask the right questions, and build a dashboard that actually helps someone make decisions.

---

## Problem I Tried to Solve

A retail company selling footwear across multiple brands and channels needs a simple way to track how the business is doing — which products/brands are profitable, which channels bring in the most sales, and how customers are responding — instead of digging through raw spreadsheets every time.

So I built this dashboard to answer:

* How is the business performing across products, brands, channels, and customers?
* Where can the business improve?

---

## A Few Things I Noticed

* Sports Shoes generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
* The Online sales channel outperformed Store and Referral Program.
* Returning customers contributed a significant share of total revenue.
* Premium products generated the highest revenue among all product classes.
* Customer satisfaction remained consistently high (~90%) across most categories.

---

## What I Learned

* Structuring data using fact and dimension tables before building dashboards.
* Writing DAX measures for KPIs such as Profit Margin and Customer Satisfaction Rate.
* Designing dashboards that are clean, interactive, and easy to understand.
* Thinking from a business stakeholder's perspective instead of focusing only on technical implementation.
